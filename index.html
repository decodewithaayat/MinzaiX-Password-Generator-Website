// 04 — FIELD MANUAL

Security tips from the vault

16+
Go long, not complex

Every extra character multiplies crack time. 16+ chars with full charset = centuries to brute-force.

≠
Unique per site

One breach shouldn't unlock your whole life. Generate a fresh password for every account.

◈
Use a manager

Humans can't memorize 100 strong passwords. Bitwarden, 1Password or KeePassXC can.

2FA
Enable 2FA always

Even a strong password can leak. A second factor (authenticator / key) stops 99% of takeovers.
